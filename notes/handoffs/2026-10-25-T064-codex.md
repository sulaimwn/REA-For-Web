  and server Node installations remain unchanged; services use the private runtime.

- Fresh real Electron ASAR and .NET fixtures. Historical evidence remains
  untouched. Electron is 28 nodes/60 links: upstream removed one duplicate
  preload mapping; all three bridge-to-channel paths remain intact.
- REA 6.8 now projects internal managed calls with site counts/opcode histograms.
  Fold matching member-inspection calls into projected links, preserving their
  IDs, evidence and counts; keep external API enrichment. Retain upstream partial
  coverage and omission limits. The optional no-members case still shows projected
  calls honestly. Legacy 6.4–6.7 outputs continue through their existing path.
