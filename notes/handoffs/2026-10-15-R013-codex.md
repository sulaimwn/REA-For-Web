## Android audit / follow-ups

Read-only second review by `/root/r012_final_review` confirms:

- Full APK hashing in `AndroidAnalysisService -> BinaryTargetResolver` and again
  in `JadxProvider` remains, plus JAR/bridge hashing. Provider/internal operation
  deadlines remain 120 seconds (initial connection 30 seconds). No new method
  batching; no large-APK speed improvement established.
- New `direct_invokes` / exhaustiveness data is not projected by our adapter.
- Duplicate class names now have explicit inventory. Selecting an ambiguous
  class name is refused, rather than safely yielding an arbitrary merged class.
- **Existing truthfulness defect worth a fixture-backed fix:**
  `packages/rea-adapter/src/android.ts:354-366` counts unavailable bodies but
  ignores `method.source.status === 'partial'` on available bodies. Upstream
  `JadxProtocol.js:120-128` uses that status for truncation. An otherwise uncapped
  bundle can therefore claim complete coverage despite truncated source. This
  predates 6.5.0; do not silently call it a new engine regression.
