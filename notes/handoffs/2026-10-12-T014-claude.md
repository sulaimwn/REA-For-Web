## Why this app
GitHub Desktop mostly shows gaps (dynamic channels, no bridge). VS Code's main bundle imports `electron` as an ES
namespace (which REA handles), so the demo shows **3 BrowserWindows, 4 contextBridge APIs, 27 IPC channels (18 named,
e.g. `vscode:fetchShellEnv`, `vscode:browserView:*`)**. Honest limits: main-process handlers go through VS Code's
validating wrapper, so they're dynamic and 0 channels pair. Bridge members and window preloads are passed as
variables, so they're unresolved.
