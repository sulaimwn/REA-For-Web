## What changed
- **`packages/ask/src/free.ts`:**
  - New exported `wireBytes(text)` = UTF-8 bytes after JSON escaping (without quotes). It's the size a
    message actually adds to the request body. The browser loop's budget and `fit()` use it.
  - `maxTotalBytes` goes from 8,000 to **36,000**. The old cap held only one or two tool results, so free
    answers stopped early.
  - The math: 36 KB of content plus at most 24 messages' JSON overhead (under 1 KB) stays inside the route's
    64 KiB body limit, escapes included. Even dense multilingual text (at most ~0.5 tokens per byte, about 18k
    tokens) leaves room in the 24k-token context for the system prompt, tools and a 1,024-token answer.
    `maxTotalChars` (48,000) and `maxMessageChars` (6,000) are unchanged.
- **`apps/web/lib/free-ask.ts`:** the route counts the same `wireBytes`. Before, it used raw UTF-8, which
  undercounted escapes: 6,000 `\u0000` characters are 6 KB raw but 36 KB as sent.
- **`apps/web/wrangler.jsonc`:** `"ai": { "binding": "AI" }` is enabled at the top level only. The `offline`
  preview environment still has no AI binding, because bindings aren't inherited. The comment records the
  plan check and says to remove the binding *before* any upgrade to Workers Paid.
- **Docs:** the `docs/deploy-cloudflare.md` free-tier bullet now says it's on for reaforweb.com, and that forks
  should confirm their own plan. The README's Ask section lists the three ways to ask: free (Llama 3.3 70B,
  daily limit), your own Anthropic key, or your Claude subscription through the MCP server.
