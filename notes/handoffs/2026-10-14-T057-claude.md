# T057 — Realistic input limits for real apps (claude)

**Branch:** `claude/T057-input-limits` (from main `3b3bb29`)
**Status:** review

## Why
The human uploaded WhatsApp's APK to the live beta. It failed before analysis with `Analysis failed (input_limit_exceeded)`.
Preflight counted every APK ZIP entry (images, layouts, resources) as an "app file" against a 5,000 cap, and big
real apps exceed that. The human wants limits that let real apps through.

## What changed
- **APK entries (`apps/worker/src/preflight.ts`):** they no longer count as app files. New
  `Budget.archiveEntry(expanded)` counts entries and adds expanded bytes to the unpacked-byte budget. The bounds
  that actually stop abuse are unchanged:
  - the ZIP format: at most 65,534 entries without ZIP64, and ZIP64 is refused;
  - the 8 MiB central-directory cap;
  - `maxInputBytes` on unpacked size;
  - the strict ZIP metadata validation, memory, CPU and the deadline.
  - `preflightTarget` also returns `entries`.
- **File cap (`limits.ts`):** the default `maxFiles` goes from 5,000 to **20,000**, the existing reviewed maximum,
  for ASAR and folder inputs. `maxFiles` only affects admission; it isn't passed to REA.
- **New operator settings in `queueLimitsFromEnv`** (operator-only; upload requests still can't change limits):
  `REA_ANALYSIS_MEMORY_MB`, `REA_ANALYSIS_HEAP_MB`, `REA_ANALYSIS_MAX_FILES` and `REA_ANALYSIS_MAX_INPUT_BYTES`. All
  are validated by the existing `resolveLimits` ranges (memory 512–8192, heap 64–4096 and at least 256 below memory,
  files 1–20,000, bytes up to 1 GiB). No range was widened.
- **Readable failures (`packages/pipeline/src/server.ts`):** `input_limit_exceeded`, `out_of_memory` and
  `output_limit_exceeded` now read as plain sentences instead of the raw code. The messages don't quote numbers,
  because operators can change the limits.
- **Docs:** a `docs/uploads.md` table of the settings, plus a suggested starting point for the 8 GB Netcup host.

## How to verify
- Root typecheck passes. **Worker 44/44.** **Pipeline 34/34**, with 1 new test for the three messages.
  `npm test --workspaces --if-present`: **268 tests, 267 pass, 0 fail**; the skip is the host JADX test.
- **New worker coverage:**
  - a synthetic **6,000-entry** stored ZIP APK, shaped like WhatsApp, passes preflight with `entries: 6000`;
  - the tiny APK passes even with `maxFiles: 1`;
  - unpacked-byte bombs and broken ZIP metadata are still refused;
  - default `maxFiles` is 20,000;
  - operator settings are applied (`--memory=4096m`), and out-of-range values are refused, including heap = memory.

## For Codex (deployment)
- Suggested starting point on the 8 GB, 4-vCPU host (one job at a time):
  `REA_ANALYSIS_CPUS=2 REA_ANALYSIS_MEMORY_MB=5120 REA_ANALYSIS_HEAP_MB=4096 REA_ANALYSIS_TIMEOUT_MS=1800000
  REA_ANALYSIS_MAX_INPUT_BYTES=1073741824`.
  - That leaves about 3 GB for the web app, Redis, Caddy and the OS.
  - The upload size cap (`MAX_UPLOAD_BYTES`, 512 MB) is separate and unchanged.
- **Not verified on a real WhatsApp APK** (I don't have the file). Even past admission, a very large APK may hit
  JADX memory or the deadline. The settings above give it the best chance, and failures now read clearly.
